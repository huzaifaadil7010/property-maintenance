# Google Stitch Prompt — SaaS Landing Page

Create **three separate, complete, responsive landing-page design variations** for **Property Maintenance**, a B2B SaaS for small and growing residential property portfolios. Do not combine the variations into one page.

## Product and conversion goal

Property Maintenance connects property owners, residents, and technicians through one accountable workflow—from issue reporting and technician assignment to work completion and resident confirmation. Owners also manage properties, units, occupancies, technicians, requests, and organization activity from one place.

Target the property owner as the buyer. Emphasize clarity, faster coordination, portfolio visibility, and accountability. Use **Create Account** as the primary CTA and **Log In** as the secondary CTA.

## Required page content

Each variation should include:

- A simple header with brand, section links, Log In, and Create Account.
- A benefit-led hero with concise copy and a credible product-interface visual.
- A problem/value section contrasting fragmented calls, messages, and spreadsheets with one organized workflow.
- Core capabilities: portfolio overview, property and unit management, resident issue reporting with photos, technician assignment, job progress, completion evidence, resident approval, and activity history.
- A clear five-step workflow: **Report → Assign → Start Work → Complete → Confirm**.
- A section showing how owners, residents, and technicians work together through focused role-based experiences.
- Clearly labeled placeholders for customer logos, a testimonial, a proof metric, and pricing. Do not invent company names, quotes, customer counts, performance numbers, plan names, or prices.
- A strong final Create Account CTA and a compact footer.

## Three visual variations

1. **Product Command Center:** product-led and highly scannable, with a split hero, polished dashboard mockup, operational stat cards, request rows, and structured feature grids.
2. **Editorial Property Confidence:** premium and spacious, with strong typography, warm architectural/property imagery, restrained product UI inserts, and an elegant editorial rhythm. Avoid luxury-real-estate clichés.
3. **Workflow in Motion:** story-led and more dynamic, centered on the maintenance lifecycle with a visual timeline, connected owner/resident/technician panels, and progressive workflow cards.

## Visual direction

Use a **light, warm-premium SaaS aesthetic**: warm off-white canvas, white cards, deep green-tinted ink, emerald/teal brand actions, muted sage neutrals, subtle warm borders, soft green-tinted shadows, generous spacing, and comfortably rounded corners. Use calm semantic accents—amber/orange for attention, cyan/indigo for active work, green for completion, and rose for reopened issues. Typography should feel modern, confident, and human, similar to Instrument Sans. Use clean line icons and subtle interaction cues.

Keep the result polished, trustworthy, accessible, and operationally clear. Avoid dark themes, loud gradients, glassmorphism, excessive decoration, generic corporate blue, cartoonish illustrations, dense enterprise layouts, or unsupported AI/automation claims.

Design every variation for both desktop and mobile. Use concise real copy based only on the capabilities above, not lorem ipsum. This is a public marketing page—not an internal dashboard—even when product UI is shown.
